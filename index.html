Let's clear things up

1. What is your typical turnaround time?

Typically, we deliver the first concepts within 24 to 48 hours depending on the complexity of the request.

2. What do you need from me to get started?

We need your video title, raw assets (if any), preferred style references, and a brief description of the video topic.

3. Do you offer a bulk discount for multiple thumbnails?

Yes, we offer special packages for creators looking for consistent design work over a series of videos.

4. What services do you offer as a graphic designer?

While specializing in thumbnails, we also design channel banners, stream overlays, and full branding packages.

5. How do I send you my ideas or references?

You can share them via our secure client portal, email, or a shared Google Drive/Dropbox folder.

6. How many revisions are included?

We typically include up to 3 rounds of minor revisions to ensure you are completely satisfied with the final result.
